🔥 About Fires in Portugal

Portugal experiences frequent rural fires every year, affecting forests, scrublands, and agricultural areas.

The main causes include natural events, human negligence, and sometimes intentional actions.

Understanding these patterns helps in planning prevention strategies and minimizing damage.

Visualizations in this dashboard show trends by year, district, and fire type to give a comprehensive view.
